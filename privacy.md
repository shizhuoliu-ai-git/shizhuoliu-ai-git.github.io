# Privacy policy - rclone NAS upload

rclone NAS upload is a personal, single-user tool. Only its owner uses it, to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

- It asks Google only for access to the Drive files it creates itself (the `drive.file` permission). It cannot see any other files.
- It collects, stores, sells and shares no personal data. There is no server: rclone runs on the owner's own computer and talks directly to Google.
- Nothing is passed to any third party.
